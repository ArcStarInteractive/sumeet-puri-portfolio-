Distinguished Educational Steward

Mr. Sumeet Puri is a dedicated educationist and academic leader with a strong commitment to the growth and development of school education.

As Director of Jagat Jyoti Schools and Principal of Modern Jagat Jyoti Senior Secondary School, he has been instrumental in strengthening academic standards, institutional practices, and the overall learning environment across classrooms and campuses.

His educational leadership focuses on academic excellence, discipline, value-based education, holistic development, and progressive teaching-learning practices. He believes wholeheartedly in empowering educators, nurturing students’ potential, and creating schools that prepare young learners not only for academic success but also for responsible and meaningful lives.

Beyond school governance, he has also served as the Former President of Bharat Vikas Parishad, a voluntary organisation dedicated to Sampark (Contact), Seva (Selfless Service), and Sanskar (Values), working extensively for social welfare, community service, and nation-building through diverse youth and community initiatives.
